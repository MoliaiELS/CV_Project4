\section{Unposed full-versus-sparse reconstruction}
\label{sec:part2}
We implement a custom VGGT-initialized unposed pipeline~\cite{wang2025vggt}, followed by fixed-camera 3D Gaussian Splatting~\cite{kerbl2023gaussians}. Full and sparse branches independently estimate cameras and geometry from their permitted training RGB. Reference cameras are used only for evaluation.
\paragraph{Protocol.} We retain every tenth original Waymo frame and every thirtieth DL3DV/RealEstate10K frame, excluding fixed held-out indices. Full/sparse training counts are 174/20, 267/11 and 244/10; test counts are 25, 39 and 35. Both conditions use 7,000 updates, seed 42, a 100k initial-point cap and no camera optimization.

\paragraph{Results.} Full improves PSNR and SSIM in all scenes, with a 3.13 dB PSNR gain on DL3DV. Waymo Sparse has lower LPIPS on all 25 test frames. Sparse has lower paired camera-center ATE in every scene; this does not establish better intrinsics, orientation, scene support or novel-view quality.
\paragraph{Convergence.} We evaluate saved checkpoints at 500, 1,000, 2,000, 4,000 and 7,000 updates and retain the preselected final endpoint. Intermediate test peaks are diagnostic, not model-selection criteria. Fixed cameras imply constant ATE throughout GS training.

\paragraph{Failure analysis.} We compare aligned trajectories and accumulated Gaussian alpha at the same test views. Alpha is a projected-support proxy, not true 3D completeness: opaque incorrect surfaces may score highly. Failure panels select the largest Full-minus-Sparse PSNR gap with all test frames retained in scoring. Re10k's roughly 20\% focal bias is shared by the independent Part 1 initialization and is not corrected using reference parameters.

\paragraph{Full and sparse runtime.} On the RTX A6000 course server, Full and Sparse initialization took 668.0/113.7 s on Waymo, 843.1/81.8 s on DL3DV and 931.8/71.7 s on RealEstate10K. The corresponding total measured stage times were 1322.4/901.2 s, 1198.5/273.7 s and 1459.4/427.3 s. Totals sum the initialization, training, rendering and evaluation wall times. Waymo Sparse spent longer in training, although the shorter initialization reduced the total.
\paragraph{Engineering and limitations.} A separate adapter reuses CPU-head-feature offload and verifies camera parity with the original CPU/disk initialization. Historical/new wall-time ratios are not controlled repeated benchmarks. We preserve checkpoint-bound source fingerprints and original paired results. These observations cover one seed and three scenes; no generative Part 3 gain is claimed.

Initialization time changes from 926 to 228 s (Re10k-1), 661 to 141 s (Waymo-405841), 836 to 230 s (Dl3dv-2); peak allocated GPU memory is 15.8--21.0 GiB. The exported cameras and initial point clouds pass parity checks.
